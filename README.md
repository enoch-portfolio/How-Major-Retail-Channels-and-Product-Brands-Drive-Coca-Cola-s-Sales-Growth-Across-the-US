# How are major retail channels and product brands driving Coca-Cola's sales growth across the US?

I analyzed a dataset covering over 9,600 beverage transactions to evaluate brand performance, retailer distribution channels, and year-over-year revenue trends across US regions.

## Key Metrics & High-Level Findings:

●  Gross Revenue Analyzed: $12,016,665.00 generated across 24,788,610 total units sold.

●  Year-Over-Year Growth: Sales increased from $2.42M in 2022 to $9.59M in 2023, a variance of +$7.17M.

●  Top Retailers: BevCo ($2.33M) and FizzyCo ($2.26M) led sales in 2023, while Target experienced massive year-over-year volume expansion.

●  Brand Performance: Coca-Cola led product sales ($2.27M in 2023), followed closely by Dasani Water ($1.92M) and Diet Coke ($1.63M).

●  Profitability & Margins: Achieved a total operating profit of $4,722,496.77 with a strong        average operating margin of 42.3%.

## Tools Used:

●  Excel & Pivot Tables: Data cleaning, aggregation, and YoY variance modeling.

●  Interactive Dashboards: Dynamic slicers for filtering by Invoice Date, Region, and State.
